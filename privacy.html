Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

"YouTube Agent Studio" is an internal tool used only by the owners of The Practical Click YouTube channel to manage their own channel: uploading and scheduling videos, setting thumbnails, reading the channel's analytics and moderating comments on the channel's videos.

It uses the YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

The tool accesses only the channel owners' own YouTube account. It does not collect, sell or share personal data of anyone else. Public comments on the channel's videos are read only to moderate and reply to them, and are not shared outside the team. Access tokens are stored as encrypted secrets and can be revoked at any time at Google account permissions.
